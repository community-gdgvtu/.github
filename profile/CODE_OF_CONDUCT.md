# Code of Conduct — GDG VTU

## Our Pledge

GDG on Campus VTU Belagavi is committed to providing a welcoming, harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of experience level, gender identity and expression, sexual orientation, disability, physical appearance, body size, race, age, religion, or nationality.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Being respectful and considerate toward members, mentors, speakers, and organizers.
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback.
- Crediting others' work and contributions appropriately.
- Being patient with beginners and first-time contributors.

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, discriminatory jokes, or derogatory comments in any community space (events, repos, chat groups).
- Personal attacks, trolling, or insulting/derogatory remarks.
- Publishing others' private information without consent.
- Plagiarizing or maliciously altering another contributor's work.
- Any conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional or academic setting.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all GDG VTU spaces — repositories, issues, pull requests, workshops, hackathons, Discord/WhatsApp groups, and in-person events — and when an individual is officially representing the community in public spaces.

## Enforcement

Instances of unacceptable behavior may be reported to the current organizing team (see **Organizers** in the [README](./README.md)). All reports will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly, with confidentiality respected wherever possible.

Organizers may take any action deemed appropriate, including a warning, temporary suspension from community spaces, or permanent removal from the community and associated repositories.
